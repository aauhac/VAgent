# Vocal Quality Rating Guide (non-medical)

Internal perceptual annotation for Vocal Quality benchmarking.
Inspired by clinical voice-quality concepts but **not** a GRBAS/CAPE-V clinical instrument.

Do **not** rate pitch accuracy or rhythm accuracy.

## Scale 0–4

| Score | Meaning |
|------:|---------|
| 0 | Absent / not noticeable |
| 1 | Mild / occasional |
| 2 | Clear / repeated |
| 3 | Strong |
| 4 | Very strong / dominant |

## Dimensions

### Breathiness
Perceived airy / whispery quality mixed into phonation.

### Strain / pressed-like quality
Perceived tight, compressed, effortful timbre (not “loudness”).

### Roughness
Perceived irregular / harsh / unstable quality.

### Resonance openness / balance
Descriptive spectral balance (dark/bright, congested vs open). Prefer descriptive notes; avoid “good/bad voice”.

### Onset abruptness
How abruptly phonation starts (soft → abrupt).

### Transition disruption
Breaks / instability around large pitch-range changes (not intonation accuracy).

## Rater rules

1. Blind filenames / skill labels when possible.
2. Prefer the same clip policy as the product (15–60s or vocal-active ~45s).
3. Two raters when possible; record confidence (low/medium/high).
4. No medical claims in notes.
