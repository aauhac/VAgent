# Discrimination Benchmark — Human Rating Guide

> **Role (2026 product direction):** this skill/performance discrimination benchmark is **secondary**.
> Primary validation for product claims is the **Perceptual Vocal Quality Benchmark**
> (`docs/vocal-quality-rating-guide.md`). Keep this path for Performance Supplement checks.

This guide is for **internal labeled evaluation** of Song Performance features.
It is **not** a user-facing report and **not** a medical assessment.

Do **not** rate:
- pitch accuracy vs reference melody
- rhythm / timing accuracy
- lyric correctness

Rate only vocal-production characteristics aligned with VAgent axes.

## Scale (1–5)

| Score | Meaning |
|------:|---------|
| 1 | Frequently breaks down / weak / unstable for this axis |
| 2 | Below typical amateur level |
| 3 | Typical / average |
| 4 | Clearly skilled in most sections |
| 5 | Consistently strong across most of the clip |

Use the same clip policy as the product when possible (15–60s, or deterministic vocal-active ~45s for longer files).

---

## Stability (발성 안정성)

How steadily sustained tones and regions hold pitch center and level (without judging “in tune” vs a song).

| Score | Guidance |
|------:|----------|
| 1 | Sustains / regions often collapse; wobble or level drop is frequent |
| 3 | Typical amateur stability |
| 5 | Most sections hold steadily |

---

## Projection (목소리 전달력)

Perceived clarity / forward presence of the voice (acoustic delivery traits), not loudness alone.

| Score | Guidance |
|------:|----------|
| 1 | Voice often buried or thin; delivery traits weak |
| 3 | Typical |
| 5 | Clear, stable delivery in most sections |

---

## Resonance (공명 균형)

Perceived spectral balance consistency (not “beautiful tone” aesthetics alone).

| Score | Guidance |
|------:|----------|
| 1 | Balance often extreme (overly dark/harsh/nasal swings) |
| 3 | Typical |
| 5 | Balanced and stable across sections |

---

## Dynamic control (강약 컨트롤)

Intentional, controlled loudness shaping — not mastering loudness of a commercial mix.

| Score | Guidance |
|------:|----------|
| 1 | Dynamics feel random, flat, or uncontrolled |
| 3 | Typical |
| 5 | Intentional and stable dynamic shaping |

---

## Overall skill (종합 발성 숙련)

Holistic vocal-production skill on this clip (not star ranking). Average of axes is allowed as a check, but raters may weight holistically.

## Rules

1. Prefer **blind** rating (hide group labels / filenames that reveal status).
2. At least **two raters** when possible.
3. If uncertain, leave blank rather than guessing medical causes.
4. Commercial mastering must **not** automatically raise scores — rate the vocal performance traits you hear.
5. No physiological / medical claims in notes.
